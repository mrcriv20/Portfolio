Product designer.

I'm Mike Crivellaro — a Lead Product Designer and Head of Design with over a decade of experience building products that work at enterprise scale. I've designed for startups, government agencies, and growth-stage companies — always at the intersection of aesthetics, systems thinking, and engineering pragmatism.

My work is defined by one question: what does this product need to be trusted? Not liked. Not admired. Trusted — by the people who depend on it to do their jobs.

I work best in complex problem spaces where the constraints are real, the stakes are high, and the definition of success requires more than good-looking screens. That includes federal government systems, HR and workforce platforms, and any product where failure has consequences.
